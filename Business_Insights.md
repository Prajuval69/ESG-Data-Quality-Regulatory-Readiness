# Business Insights

1. **Coverage is the main weakness.** Overall completeness is 75.7%, materially below validity (92.8%), consistency (92.4%), and traceability (98.2%). Improving collection coverage will have more impact than broad rework of already-present records.
2. **Scope 2 is the largest required-metric gap.** Only 58.3% of expected company-years are available, followed by Scope 3 and emissions reduction targets at 66.7%.
3. **Outliers dominate the exception queue.** Eleven of 37 exception instances are IQR outliers. They should be verified against source evidence, not automatically overwritten.
4. **Remediation can be risk-based.** BluePeak Retail has the lowest weighted quality score (78.4%) and readiness score (62.4%); Nova Healthcare has the highest quality score (93.8%).
5. **Quality improved while coverage declined.** The weighted score rose from 88.1% in 2024 to 89.1% in 2025, but completeness fell from 76.4% to 75.0%. The improvement reflects better control performance among reported records rather than broader availability.
6. **Traceability remains a distinct control.** Four records lack source references even though some values may otherwise appear plausible.
7. **Critical issues require immediate evidence review.** Two negative-emissions records are classified as Critical; the classification is a portfolio risk signal, not a compliance conclusion.
