# Recommendations
| Priority | Issue | Recommendation | Expected Benefit | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scope 3 gaps | Establish a focused Scope 3 collection plan with named owners. | Better coverage and fewer late reporting gaps. | Sustainability Data Lead |
| 2 | Recurring exceptions | Automate controls before data enters reporting datasets. | Earlier defect detection and less manual review. | Data Quality Lead |
| 3 | Traceability | Make source/reference capture mandatory. | Stronger audit trail and reproducibility. | ESG Data Steward |
| 4 | Unit inconsistency | Maintain a controlled metric/unit dictionary. | Better comparability and aggregation. | ESG Master Data Owner |
| 5 | Critical records | Use severity-based exception escalation. | Faster response to material issues. | ESG Reporting Lead |
| 6 | Uneven quality | Prioritize remediation using company scores. | Risk-based allocation of effort. | Program Manager |
